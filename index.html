Our Story

At The Rustic Crumb, bread is not just food—it is patience, passion, and an ancient craft passed through generations.

Founded in 2014, our neighborhood bakery began with a 50-year-old sourdough culture and a wood-fired hearth oven. We reject shortcuts, commercial yeasts, and artificial preservatives. Each loaf takes up to 36 hours from flour mixing to stone-bake, unlocking deep wheaty complexity, an airy interior, and a blistered golden crust.

Every morning before dawn, our bakers shape sourdough batards, rustic baguettes, seeded rye, and enriched brioche by hand. We believe in wholesome ingredients, honoring the soil and farmers who nurture our heirloom grains.
